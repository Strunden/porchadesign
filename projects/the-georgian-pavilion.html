The Georgian Pavilion

The Georgian Pavilion is a dedicated entertainment and leisure space inspired by the proportions and detailing of early Georgian architecture. Designed for hosting and family gatherings, the pavilion incorporates a bespoke bar, feature fireplace, lounge seating and guest facilities, creating a sophisticated yet relaxed setting for entertaining.

Porcha Design was appointed to lead the interior design and interior architectural development throughout the project, including bespoke joinery, lighting coordination, material specification and furniture procurement. A layered palette of natural materials, architectural detailing and custom-designed elements creates a timeless interior that balances character with contemporary comfort.
