Planning a renovation, extension or new build and unsure where to start? Struggling with layouts, kitchens, bathrooms, lighting or material selections? The Interiors Power Hour is designed to help homeowners navigate design decisions with clarity and confidence. Leave with a clear action plan for your project, recommended suppliers and practical next steps.

Led by Sorcha Harman, founder of Porcha Design, whose work has been featured in IMAGE Magazine, IMAGE Interiors, House & Home Magazine and Ireland's Homes Interiors & Living, this focused one-to-one consultation provides practical, professional advice tailored to your project.

Whether you are seeking a second opinion, feeling overwhelmed by choices, or looking to avoid costly mistakes before making key decisions, the session is designed to provide clear direction and actionable next steps, without the commitment of a full interior design service.

From kitchen layouts and bathroom design to lighting, bespoke joinery, materials, finishes and furniture planning, every consultation is tailored to the specific needs of your home and project.

What can we cover?

Whether you are planning a renovation, refining a design scheme or simply feeling stuck, we can discuss:

  • Kitchen and bathroom design
  • Layouts and space planning
  • Lighting and electrical layouts
  • Bespoke joinery and storage
  • Furniture and styling
  • Material and finish selections
  • Renovations, extensions and new builds
  • Reviewing plans before construction begins
  • Any specific design challenge you are facing

How it works

Most clients only need one session to gain clarity and move forward confidently with their project.

  1. Book your session Choose a time that suits you and complete payment to secure your consultation.
  2. Send your information Share photographs, plans, inspiration images and a list of questions in advance. This allows us to make the most of our time together.
  3. Attend your consultation Receive tailored advice, recommendations and practical solutions specific to your project.
  4. Receive a follow-up summary Following the consultation, you will receive a concise email outlining the key recommendations and next steps discussed during the session.

FAQ

What is the Interiors Power Hour?

A focused one-hour interior design consultation designed to help homeowners work through design decisions, renovation plans and interior design challenges.

Can one hour really help?

Yes. Clients are often surprised by how much can be covered in a focused session. By reviewing your plans, photographs and questions in advance, we can make the most of the time and provide practical, actionable advice.

How is the Interiors Power Hour different from hiring an interior designer?

The Interiors Power Hour provides focused professional advice without the commitment of a full design service. It is ideal for homeowners who need expert guidance, reassurance or help making decisions at key stages of their project.

Is the Interiors Power Hour right for me?

The Interiors Power Hour is ideal for homeowners who need professional guidance on a renovation, extension, new build or interior project without committing to a full design service. Whether you are stuck on a specific decision or looking for an expert second opinion, the session is tailored to your project.

What can we cover during the consultation?

Anything from kitchen and bathroom design to layouts, lighting, joinery, flooring, colour schemes, furniture layouts, material selections and renovation planning. The session is entirely tailored to your priorities.

What should I send before the consultation?

Photographs, floor plans, inspiration images and a list of questions are all helpful. Don't worry if you don't have everything prepared. We'll work with whatever information is available.

Can you help with kitchens, bathrooms and bespoke joinery?

Absolutely. These are some of the most common topics discussed during the consultation. Advice can cover layouts, functionality, finishes, lighting and how different elements work together.

What if I decide I need more help afterwards?

Many clients find the consultation gives them enough clarity to move forward independently. However, if further support is required, additional design services can be discussed following the session.

Can you review plans before I start building?

Yes. Many clients book a consultation before construction begins to review layouts, identify opportunities and avoid costly mistakes later in the project.
