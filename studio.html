Studio

Sorcha Harman

Interior designer · Dublin

Sorcha Harman, founder of Porcha Design, is an interior designer based between Dublin and Berlin, working across Europe and beyond.

Her practice is rooted in luxury residential interior design: high-end residential renovations, period homes, contemporary residences and bespoke new builds, with a focus on projects that require both sensitivity and precision.

Before establishing her own studio, she built her experience at Suzie McAdam Design as Design Manager, and previously at Optimise Design, developing a strong foundation in complex residential interior design and high-end renovation projects. Alongside this, she has worked across Los Angeles, London and Dublin within established interior design studios, bringing a broad and internationally informed perspective to private residential interiors.

Textiles remain a starting point in her process rather than an afterthought. They inform atmosphere, material direction and the layering of spaces from the earliest stages of a project.

Her work moves between Georgian and Victorian townhouse renovations, large family homes and contemporary residential interiors, alongside selected hospitality projects in California, London and the Cotswolds. Her work has been featured in IMAGE Interiors, House & Home and Robb Report.

How I work

A colour narrative, then the detail.

I begin each project by developing a considered colour narrative, a guiding framework that informs architectural decisions, finishes and furnishings alike. This ensures every element feels intentional and cohesive.

Through collaborative concept development, schemes evolve into detailed drawings, sample studies and fully resolved design proposals. I oversee the complete specification of furniture, fixtures and equipment (FF&E), ensuring materials, joinery, lighting and bespoke elements are thoughtfully integrated and aligned with the project's budget and timeline.

From initial concept through to final installation, I manage the entire design journey with precision and clarity, working closely with architects, contractors and specialist craftspeople to deliver cohesive, characterful interiors.

Full-service interior design

We provide full-service interior design for high-end residential projects in Dublin and throughout Ireland.

Acting as creative lead and strategic advisor, we guide each project from early concept through to final installation.

Our approach combines thoughtful design development with strategic procurement and informed specification. Drawing on trusted suppliers and long-standing industry relationships, we source bespoke furnishings and finishes with both quality and longevity in mind.

From spatial planning and bespoke joinery detailing to full FF&E coordination and installation, we manage the layers of detail that allow a home to feel resolved, cohesive and quietly distinctive.

Porcha Design is a high-end residential interior design studio working across Dublin and throughout Ireland, specialising in considered renovations, contemporary homes and bespoke new builds. Each project is personally led by Sorcha, with a highly collaborative and detail-driven approach.
